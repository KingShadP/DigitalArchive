# Brand Governance

**KINGSHADP is the primary identity.**

This document outlines the strict rules governing the visual and narrative presentation of the KingShadP universe.

## Approved Marks & Identity Assets
1. **KingShadP Signature Wordmark**
2. **KSP Monogram**
3. **SP Crest**
4. **Giragon**
5. **Halo-Crown Motif** (Only where explicitly appropriate)

## Absolute Restrictions (NEVER Do This)
- **NEVER** create "KP" initials.
- **NEVER** invent another crest.
- **NEVER** invent another mascot.
- **NEVER** redesign or alter the Giragon.
- **NEVER** add generic king, royal, or medieval imagery.
- **NEVER** create fake luxury clichés or "premium" jargon (e.g., "Elevate your style").
- **NEVER** fabricate biographical details or achievements for the artist.
- **NEVER** create uncontrolled alternate logos for temporary campaigns.

## Categorization of Visuals
All visuals must be clearly differentiated into the following categories. Temporary campaign visuals must never redefine or replace permanent identity assets.

### 1. Permanent Brand Assets
Core identity marks (Wordmark, Giragon, Crest). These are immutable and govern the overarching property.

### 2. Campaign Art
Visuals specific to a time-bound release, collection, or product drop. Allowed to have distinct aesthetic themes but must reside *under* the permanent brand umbrella.

### 3. Editorial Art
Narrative photography, 3D renders (like the Monolith Coat or Architectural Vaults), and world-building visuals used to tell the story of an artifact.

### 4. Experimental Digital Content
Highly interactive, temporary, or generative digital pieces (e.g., WebGL experiments, audio-reactive canvases). These live in isolated environments and do not alter the main UI shell.
